About

Computer science meets
population health.

My research translates heterogeneous healthcare data into actionable insights for diagnostics, treatment, and patient outcomes. I study how machine learning can bring together longitudinal clinical records, imaging, genomics, and digital health streams.

At UTMB, my work connects multimodal modeling with data quality, fairness, and human-centered tools. Applications span cancer, chronic disease management, and mental health.

Before joining UTMB in September 2025, I served on the faculty at Meharry Medical College and directed its mHealth Wearable Sensors Lab. I completed my postdoctoral training in hematology/oncology at the University of Michigan.
